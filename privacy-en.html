Privacy Policy

Effective: October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information Crazy Fishing (환장 낚시) for Mac and iPhone (the “app”) collects, why, and how it is kept and deleted. The app is run by an individual developer (the “operator”).

In short: We use no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools. We store only what the game needs, delete it right away when you delete your account, and never sell or share it.

1. Information we collect

ItemDetailsWhen
Account identifierA random account ID for guests. With Sign in with Apple, the user identifier from Apple and, if you choose to share it, your email (or Apple’s private relay address)When you create an account
NicknameThe nickname you choose (shown in the Hall of Fame and to friends)When you set it
Game dataCatches (species, size, time), collection, aquarium, coins and pearls, gear, Hall of Fame recordsWhile you play
FriendsFriend code, friends/requests/blocked lists, invite links, whether you are fishing now and when you were last online, catch updates shown to friendsWhen you use friends
ChatMessages you send to friends, and reports you makeWhen you chat or report
PurchasesApp Store transaction ID, product, purchase/refund time. Payment details are handled by Apple; we never receive themWhen you buy pearls
Server logsIP address and request timeWhen the app talks to the server

Mac app settings, Focus status (used only to keep alerts quiet), and window size stay on your device and are never sent to the server.

2. How we use it

  • Accounts, sign-in, saving and syncing your game between Mac and iPhone
  • Judging catches on the server and preventing cheating (e.g. macros)
  • Hall of Fame, friends, and chat
  • Delivering pearls and handling refunds
  • Handling reports, protecting players, answering questions, keeping the service stable

3. Retention and deletion

  • Account and game data: until you delete your account. “Delete Account” in the app’s account settings removes it immediately and permanently.
  • Chat messages: deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Catch updates shown to friends: deleted after 1 day.
  • Invite links: expire after 7 days.
  • Reports: kept up to 1 year to handle reports and disputes.
  • Purchase records: kept for 5 years as required by Korean consumer protection law. When you delete your account, the link to your account is removed and only the transaction record remains.
  • Server logs: kept up to 90 days for security and troubleshooting.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or provide your personal information to third parties, except where required by law. We use these providers to run the service:

ProviderPurpose
Supabase, Inc.Servers, database, sign-in (data stored in the Seoul, South Korea region)
Apple Inc.Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases and refunds

Supabase, Inc. is a US company and may access data from the United States while operating and supporting the service.

5. What other players can see

  • Hall of Fame: nickname (or “Anonymous Angler”), fish size, date, gear used
  • Friends only: nickname, friend code, whether you are fishing, catch updates, chat messages
  • Share cards you choose to share may include your nickname and friend code.

Other players’ account IDs are never sent to the app.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your information, or ask us to stop processing it, at any time. Change your nickname in the app, and delete your account and all records with “Delete Account”. For anything else, email us and we will respond promptly.

7. Children

The app does not ask for real names, contact details, or location. Parents or guardians may exercise the rights above on behalf of children.

8. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Database access is restricted so players can only read their own data, and only the server can change game records.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in the device keychain.
