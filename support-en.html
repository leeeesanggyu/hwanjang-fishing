FAQ

How do I fish?

Press “Cast” in the small fishing window at the top of your Mac screen and get back to work. When the float dips, click the window or press ⌥⌘F to hook. Right-click or ⌥⌘R reels in.

How do I quit?

Click the fishing window once and press ⌘Q, or click the Crazy Fishing icon in the menu bar and choose “Quit Crazy Fishing”. When your pointer leaves the fishing window, the keyboard goes back to the app you were using.

Can I fish on iPhone?

Fishing happens on the Mac. The iPhone app shows your collection, aquarium, shop, Hall of Fame, friends, and chat. Sign in with the same account to see the same records.

How do I add friends?

Share your friend code from the Friends tab, or send an invite link — tapping it makes you friends right away. When friends are fishing, their floats appear in your pond.

Someone sent me an offensive message

Long-press (iPhone) or right-click (Mac) the message to Report or Block. Blocked players can’t see your messages or send friend requests again. Reports are reviewed by the operator.

I bought pearls but didn’t get them

The app checks again when you reopen it. If they still don’t arrive, email your App Store order number and friend code. Refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens if I change devices?

Accounts using Sign in with Apple carry over when you sign in with the same Apple ID. Guest accounts can’t be recovered after signing out or changing devices, so Sign in with Apple is recommended.

How do I delete my account?

Mac: Collection window → Account → “Delete Account”. iPhone: Settings (gear) → “Delete Account”. Your collection, aquarium, coins, pearls, and Hall of Fame records are permanently deleted.
